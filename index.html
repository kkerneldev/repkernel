How it works

Apps in this source

Each app installs through AltStore Classic with your own Apple ID.

Add the source in three steps

Works from your iPhone or iPad. AltStore Classic needs to be installed first.

  1. Tap Add to AltStore

    On your device, press the red button at the top. AltStore opens and asks you to confirm the source.

  2. Or copy the link

    In AltStore, open Sources, tap +, and paste the source URL you copied above.

  3. Get an app

    Open the Browse tab, pick an app, and tap Free. AltStore signs and installs it.

Free Apple IDs can keep three sideloaded apps active and need a refresh every 7 days. AltStore does this in the background while your device is on the same Wi-Fi as AltServer.
