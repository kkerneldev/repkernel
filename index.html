Add the source in three steps

Works from your iPhone or iPad. Your sideloading client needs to be installed first.

  1. Tap Add Source

    On your device, press the top button. Your installer opens and asks to confirm the source.

  2. Or copy the link

    In your app, open Sources, tap +, and paste the source URL you copied above.

  3. Get an app

    Open the Browse tab, pick an app, and tap Get. The app signs and installs it automatically.

Free Apple IDs can keep three sideloaded apps active and need a refresh every 7 days. AltStore does this in the background while your device is on the same Wi-Fi as AltServer.
